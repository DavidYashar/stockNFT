FAQ

Everything you need to know

add What is stockNFT?

stockNFT is an NFT collection on the Robinhood Chain that works as a smart account, allowing the holders to have tokenized real-world stocks as NFTs. Each stock NFT holds a share of the purchased stock backed by actual shares held by a regulated custodian.

add How are the stocks custodied?

Underlying shares are held by licensed, regulated custodians. Custody proofs are published on-chain and verifiable by anyone. The custodian is legally obligated to maintain 1:1 backing.

add What is the Robinhood Chain?

Robinhood Chain is a high-performance EVM-compatible blockchain designed for financial applications. It offers sub-second finality, low gas fees, and native DeFi primitives optimized for tokenized assets.

add Can I redeem my NFT for real shares?

Yes. stockNFT supports redemption of NFTs for the underlying shares through a qualified broker. Redemption is subject to the custodian's standard processing timeline (typically T+2).

add What is ERC-6551?

ERC-6551 is a token standard that allows NFTs to act as smart accounts. Each stockNFT is a token-bound account (TBA) that can hold assets like the underlying stock shares and $PILE tokens, making your NFT a self-custodial wallet on chain. Everything is traceable, redeemable, and owned entirely by you.

add What wallets are supported?

Currently, stockNFT project supports MetaMask, OKX, CoinBase, and Phantom. More wallet compatibles to come.

add Do I get dividends or voting rights?

Voting rights: No. GOOGL tokens are tokenised debt securities, not equity shares — you get economic exposure, not a seat at shareholder meetings.

Dividends: Handled automatically on-chain. When Google pays a dividend, the token's shares-per-token ratio adjusts upward via the uiMultiplier() mechanism. Your token silently accrues more underlying value — no claim button, no cash deposit. You realize the accumulated value when you sell or redeem.

add What are the fees?

On mint phase: just the gas fee on Robinhood Chain. On claim of the $PILE token, the gas fee for claim, and for the share of stock, the gas fee as well as 5% management fee.
